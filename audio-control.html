Overall sound

Changes all the music at once: how loud it is, how fast it plays, and how high or low it sounds.

The 7 music tracks

The music is made of 7 tracks playing together. Track 1 is the background and is always on. Each of the others belongs to one group of microbes at the edge of the screen, and you only hear it while those microbes are inside the bubble.
In each row: the bar shows how loud the track is right now, Mute switches it off, and Test plays it as if someone had just dropped that microbe in. Underneath, Volume, Speed and Pitch change that track alone, on top of the overall sound above. Speed 1.00× keeps the track in time with the others.

How the music follows the microbes

Dropping a microbe into the bubble brings in its track. The more microbes of that kind are in the bubble, the louder its track gets.

When someone drops a microbe in

Each new microbe gets a short highlight: its track jumps to full volume for a moment while the rest of the music gets quieter, so you can hear what just arrived. Then everything goes back to normal.

Changes apply instantly and are remembered in this browser.
